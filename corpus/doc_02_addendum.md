# ADDENDUM TO LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT
**Doc ID:** doc_02_addendum
**Relates to:** doc_01_lease_main (Leave and License Agreement dated 15th May 2007)

---

THIS ADDENDUM is made at Mumbai on this **1st day of April, 2008**, by and between M/s. Vantage Business Parks Pvt. Ltd. ("the Licensor") and M/s. Solstice Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("the Licensee"), being parties to the Leave and License Agreement dated 15th May 2007 in respect of the Premises at Meridian Corporate Towers, 4th Floor ("the Principal Agreement").

**WHEREAS** the parties wish to amend certain terms of the Principal Agreement as set out below, with all other terms and conditions of the Principal Agreement remaining unchanged and in full force and effect.

**NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

## 1. AMENDMENT TO CAR PARKING (Clause 1.1 and Schedule B of the Principal Agreement)

Effective from **1st April 2008**, the number of reserved car parking spaces granted to the Licensee is increased from **8 (eight)** to **10 (ten)**, at no additional license fee. All other terms of Clause 1.1 and Schedule B remain unchanged.

## 2. AMENDMENT TO ESCALATION (Clause 5 of the Principal Agreement)

Effective from **1st April 2008**, Clause 5 (Escalation) of the Principal Agreement is amended such that the license fee escalation on renewal shall be **12% (twelve percent)** of the last-paid license fee, in place of the previously agreed 15%. For the avoidance of doubt, this amendment applies to any escalation calculated after the date of this Addendum, including in connection with any renewal under Clause 3.

## 3. AMENDMENT TO NAME BOARD (Clause 11.4 of the Principal Agreement)

In addition to the name board permitted at the entrance of the Premises under Clause 11.4, the Licensee shall be permitted to display one additional name board at the main lobby entrance of Meridian Corporate Towers, at the Licensee's own cost, subject to applicable building rules.

## 4. NO OTHER CHANGES

Save as expressly amended by this Addendum, all other terms and conditions of the Principal Agreement dated 15th May 2007 shall continue to remain in full force and effect and binding on both parties.
