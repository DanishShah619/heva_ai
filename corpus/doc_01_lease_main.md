Clause numbering has been corrected to run sequentially (1 through 19) with consistent sub-clause numbering, since the source template had inconsistent/restarting numbering. THIS LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT ("this License Agreement") is made at Mumbai on this 15th day of May, 2007 by and between M/s. Vantage Business Parks Pvt. Ltd., incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956, having its registered office at 21 Nariman Point, Mumbai-400021, hereinafter referred to as "the Licensor," of the One Part;

AND

M/s. Solstice Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956, having its registered office at 88 Andheri Industrial Estate, Andheri (East), Mumbai-400059, hereinafter referred to as "the Licensee," of the Other Part.

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Licensor is the absolute owner of the building called **Meridian Corporate Towers**, more particularly described in Schedule A hereto ("the Schedule A Property").

B. The Licensor has obtained the necessary sanctions and permissions from the Corporation and other local authorities, including the Building & Sanction Plan and Occupancy Certificate.

C. The Licensee, being desirous of taking on license a portion of the Schedule A Property being the Unit located on the **4th Floor**, having a built-up area of **500 sq. mtrs. (5,382 sq. ft.)** and carpet area of **420 sq. mtrs. (4,521 sq. ft.)**, more particularly described in Schedule B hereto ("the said Premises"), for the purpose of carrying on its business operations, has approached the Licensor, and the Licensor has agreed to give the Premises on license.

**NOW THIS WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

## 1. GRANT OF LICENSE

**1.1** The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee a non-transferable license to use and occupy the Premises during the term of this License Agreement for its business purpose for a term of **36 months**. The Licensee shall also be entitled to use all common areas, facilities, and amenities appurtenant to the Premises, along with **8 reserved car parking spaces**, as specified under Clause 13.

**1.2** For the use and occupation of the Premises, the Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of **Rs. 4,50,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand only)**, in advance before the 10th day of each calendar month, after deducting tax at source as per the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## 2. LICENSE TERM

**2.1** The month of the license shall be as per the English calendar month.

**2.2** The License Agreement shall be operative for a period of **36 (thirty-six) months** commencing from the **1st day of June, 2007** (the "Effective Date") up to the **31st day of May, 2010** (the "License Term").

## 3. RENEWAL

**3.1** Renewal at the end of the License Term shall be at the option of both parties. If the Licensee intends to renew, it shall give the Licensor written notice of its intention. Such notice shall be deemed given unless a notice of intention to vacate is given in writing by the Licensee to the Licensor **six months prior** to expiry of the License Term.

**3.2** If the Licensee exercises its option to renew, the Licensor shall execute a fresh License Agreement at the Licensee's cost. The renewal transaction shall be completed within **30 days** from the date of expiry of the License Term, and in the interim the Licensee shall be allowed to continue occupying the Premises, subject to payment of a license fee of a **minimum 15% more** than the prior license fee.

## 4. LICENSE FEE

**4.1** The Licensee shall pay the license fee of Rs. 4,50,000/- per month on or before the 10th day of every month, subject to statutory deductions. The Licensor shall grant **8 reserved car parking spaces** free of additional license fee.

**4.2** In the event of non-payment of the License Fee within **7 days** from the due date, the Licensor shall be paid interest at **18% p.a.** on the unpaid amount until actual payment.

**4.3** In case of default of payment for **2 consecutive months**, the Licensor shall give **15 days' notice** to the Licensee to make payment together with interest at 18% p.a. If the Licensee fails to pay within 15 days of such notice, the Licensor may terminate this License Agreement and re-enter the Premises. The Licensor shall refund the security deposit after deducting all dues.

## 5. ESCALATION

The license fee shall be enhanced by **15%** on the last-paid license fee after **12 months**, if the License Term is extended in terms hereof.

## 6. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**6.1** The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor a refundable, interest-free security deposit of **Rs. 27,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Seven Lakh only)**, receipt of which the Licensor acknowledges. The Security Deposit shall be refunded after deducting any dues towards unpaid electricity, telephone, and broadband bills at the time of vacating the Premises. In the absence of such bills, the Licensor shall withhold **1.5 times** the amount calculated on the average of the last 4 billing cycles.

**6.2** On expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement, the Licensor shall refund the Security Deposit without interest, simultaneously with the Licensee vacating the Premises (reasonable wear and tear and force-majeure damage excepted).

**6.3** If the Licensor does not refund the Security Deposit in full at the time of receiving vacant possession: (i) the Licensee shall not be obliged to vacate and may continue to use the Premises without paying license fee until the deposit is refunded in full, and (ii) the Licensor shall be liable to pay the Licensee interest at **12% p.a.** on the outstanding Security Deposit from the date of termination or expiry until the date of actual refund.

## 7. REPAIRS

**7.1** The Licensee agrees to undertake all day-to-day minor repairs. For major repairs (e.g., leakage, seepage, bursting of sanitary pipes, or structural damp), the Licensor shall repair the same within a reasonable period at the Licensor's own cost.

**7.2** If the Licensor fails to repair or maintain the Premises within **15 days** of receiving written notice from the Licensee, or in case of emergency, the Licensee may repair the same at its own cost and reimburse itself by producing authentic bills, verified by the Licensor.

## 8. TAXES & OUTGOINGS

**8.1** The Licensor shall bear and pay all property taxes and outgoings in respect of the Premises, including maintenance of the building and common areas.

**8.2** If the Licensor fails to pay any taxes or outgoings under Clause 8.1, the Licensee may, at its discretion, pay the same, and the Licensor shall reimburse the Licensee within **30 days** of receiving documentary proof of payment.

## 9. LICENSEE'S COVENANTS

**9.1** The Licensee covenants to: (a) pay the License Fee without default, subject to TDS; (b) use the Premises solely as its office and storage-cum-assembly unit for assembly and testing of its products; (c) maintain the Premises in good order; (d) promptly pay all electricity charges based on its dedicated meter; (e) observe all rules and bylaws of the Government of Maharashtra; (f) not cause nuisance to other occupants; (g) on termination, deliver the Premises in the condition consistent with these covenants (normal wear and tear, and damage from fire not caused by Licensee negligence, riots, earthquake, storm, war, terrorism, labour unrest, civil commotion, or acts of God, excepted); (h) provide access to the Licensor's authorized representatives during business hours upon at least **1 business day's** prior notice; (i) hand over duplicate keys to the Licensor on expiry or earlier termination; (j) occupy the Premises at its own risk, with the Licensor not liable for theft, loss, or injury; (k) not be entitled to keys of the main compound gate; (l) indemnify the Licensor against liabilities arising from the Licensee's breach or disputes with its own employees.

## 10. LICENSOR'S COVENANTS

**10.1** The Licensor covenants and warrants: (a) **Quiet Enjoyment** — to permit the Licensee to peacefully hold and enjoy the Premises without interruption during the License Term; (b) **Full Power and Authority** — that the Licensor is the absolute and lawful owner, and the Premises are free of mortgages, encumbrances, liens, or charges; (c) **Commercial Use** — that the Premises are zoned for commercial use and all necessary approvals have been obtained; (d) **Sanction Plans** — that the building complies with approved Municipal Corporation building plans permitting commercial use; (e) **Property Taxes** — that the Licensor shall pay all property taxes per Clause 8; (f) **Structural Repairs** — that the Licensor shall carry out all major/structural repairs and waterproofing works without inconveniencing the Licensee; (g) **No Litigation** — that there is no pending claim, action, or litigation relating to the Premises; (h) **Indemnity** — the Licensor indemnifies the Licensee against costs arising from title disputes or breach of the Licensor's warranties.

## 11. RIGHTS OF THE LICENSEE

**11.1 Alterations, Modifications and Additions** — Subject to submitting required drawings to the Licensor, the Licensee may install fittings and fixtures (air-conditioners, sun blinders, electrical switches, lights, fans, carpets, partitions, cabins, computers, telephone exchange, etc.) at its own cost; these remain the Licensee's property. On expiry/termination, the Licensee may remove moveable improvements (chairs, tables, modular partitions, AC equipment, generator, UPS, EPABX) but must restore the Premises to its original condition; no structural changes are permitted. Fixed improvements (internal ducting, wiring, bathroom fittings) may be left behind. The Licensee may renovate at its own expense with the Licensor's prior permission, provided it does not adversely affect the Premises. The Licensee may erect internal walls/wooden partitions but no permanent external structures without the Licensor's written consent.

**11.2 Access to Premises** — The Licensor shall ensure the Licensee has clear, uninterrupted access to the Premises **7 days a week**.

**11.3 Condition of Premises** — The Licensee shall be entitled to return the Premises in the condition it was in at the time of occupation (subject to Clause 9.1(g)).

**11.4 Name Board** — The Licensee may display name boards only at the entrance of the Premises, at its own cost, in accordance with applicable rules.

**11.5 Premises Unfit for Occupation** — If the Premises are destroyed or rendered unfit for occupation due to a force majeure event, the Licensee shall notify the Licensor in writing. If the Licensor fails to repair the Premises within **15 days** of such notice, the Licensee may terminate this Agreement immediately. The Licensee shall not be liable for License Fee during the period the Premises are unfit for occupation, unless the Licensee continues to occupy the Premises despite the unfitness, in which case the License Fee remains payable.

## 12. TERMINATION

**12.1** Neither party may terminate this Agreement before the expiry of the first **12 months** from the Effective Date (the "Lock-In Period"), except for termination under Clause 4.3 (default in payment). If either party serves notice of termination before the expiry of the Lock-In Period (other than under Clause 4.3), the terminating party shall reimburse the other party liquidated damages equivalent to the License Fee for the balance of the Lock-In Period.

**12.2** In the event of default by the Licensor in respect of any covenant herein, including misrepresentation or breach, the Licensee may terminate this Agreement by giving **30 days' written notice**.

**12.3** The Licensor may terminate this Agreement only if the Licensee commits a material breach. The Licensor shall first give the Licensee **one month's notice** in writing to rectify the breach. If the Licensee fails to rectify within this period, the Licensor may terminate by giving a further **one month's notice**, and the Licensee shall vacate and hand over peaceful possession; failing which, the Licensee shall pay liquidated damages of **Rs. 15,000/- per day** to the Licensor for continued occupation after the termination date.

**12.4** The Licensee shall obtain, at its own cost, all licenses, permissions, and No Objection Certificates required under law to run its activities from the Premises; the Licensor shall sign any requisite forms/applications for procurement of such licenses.

## 13. TRANSFER OF PREMISES

The Licensor shall not dispose of the Premises during the License Term except after informing the transferee of this License Agreement, ensuring the Licensee's rights are not prejudiced. If the Licensor transfers the Premises, it shall cause the transferee to execute a fresh License Agreement with the Licensee on the same terms.

## 14. JURISDICTION

Any dispute relating to this License Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of Courts at Mumbai.

## 15. NOTICE

Any notice under this Agreement shall be deemed given if sent by registered post acknowledgement due, to:

**Licensor:** M/s. Vantage Business Parks Pvt. Ltd., 21 Nariman Point, Mumbai-400021
**Licensee:** M/s. Solstice Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., 88 Andheri Industrial Estate, Andheri (East), Mumbai-400059

## 16. BROKERAGE

The Licensor and Licensee shall each pay brokerage to their respective Real Estate Consultant separately; such payment has no connection to this License Agreement.

## 17. STAMP DUTY AND LEGAL COST

Stamp duty, registration charges, and other miscellaneous expenses regarding this License Agreement shall be borne by the Licensee. Each party shall bear its own legal advisors' fees.

## 18. AMENDMENT

No modification or amendment of this License Agreement, and no waiver of any term, shall be valid unless made in writing and duly executed by both Parties.

## 19. WAIVER

No waiver or acquiescence of any breach, or any continuing or subsequent breach, of any provision of this License Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of any right arising out of this License Agreement.
